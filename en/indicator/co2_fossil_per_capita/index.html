CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry)

Fossil and industry CO2 emissions per person (t CO2 per capita), as published by the Global Carbon Project (using its own population series). Different from World Bank EN.ATM.CO2E.PC (archived) and EN.GHG.CO2.PC.CE.AR5 (EDGAR).

View CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) on the globe

Definition

Fossil and industry CO2 emissions per person (t CO2 per capita), as published by the Global Carbon Project (using its own population series). Different from World Bank EN.ATM.CO2E.PC (archived) and EN.GHG.CO2.PC.CE.AR5 (EDGAR).

Unit: t CO2/person

How to read this indicator

CO2 emissions from burning fossil fuels and from cement production and similar sources, divided by population. The unit is tonnes of CO2 per person.

How it differs from similar indicators

Total emissions depend on the size (population) of a country, and the per-capita value evens that out. The older World Bank series (EN.ATM.CO2E.PC) and series derived from EDGAR have a different source and definition, and their values do not match.

World total over time

The time series of the world aggregate (as aggregated by the World Bank and others). It is not always the simple sum of the country values.

Reading notes

  • It is divided by the population used by the Global Carbon Project, so it can differ slightly from a value calculated with World Bank population. It is an average, so differences within a country are not visible.
  • Countries and years without a value are "No data". They are not filled with 0 or estimates.
  • Providers sometimes revise past values. This site shows the values as fetched at the time of the dataset version.
