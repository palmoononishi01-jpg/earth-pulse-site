Forest area (% of land area)

Land under natural or planted stands of trees of at least 5 meters, as a share of land area (excludes trees in agricultural production and urban parks).

View Forest area (% of land area) on the globe

Definition

Land under natural or planted stands of trees of at least 5 meters, as a share of land area (excludes trees in agricultural production and urban parks).

Unit: %

How to read this indicator

The share (%) of land area covered by forest. Forest means land of at least 0.5 hectares where trees taller than 5 meters cover at least 10 percent.

How it differs from similar indicators

It is a share, not the area of forest itself (km²). The denominator is land area, which excludes inland water such as lakes and large rivers, not the whole territory.

Reading notes

  • Trees in agricultural production and urban parks are not included. Definitions and survey methods differ by country, and the values are estimates based on surveys taken every few years.
  • Countries and years without a value are "No data". They are not filled with 0 or estimates.
  • Providers sometimes revise past values. This site shows the values as fetched at the time of the dataset version.
