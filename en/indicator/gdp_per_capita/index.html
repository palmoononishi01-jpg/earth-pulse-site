GDP per capita (current US$)

GDP divided by midyear population (current US$).

View GDP per capita (current US$) on the globe

Definition

GDP divided by midyear population (current US$).

Unit: US dollars

How to read this indicator

GDP divided by midyear population, in nominal US dollars. It shows the average size of the economy per person.

How it differs from similar indicators

GDP is the size of a country's economy, while GDP per capita is the average per person. It does not describe individual incomes or how income is distributed. It is also different from values based on purchasing power parity (PPP), which this site does not use.

Reading notes

  • It is an average, so it does not show inequality within a country. In countries with small populations, a few industries or companies can move the value a lot.
  • Countries and years without a value are "No data". They are not filled with 0 or estimates.
  • Providers sometimes revise past values. This site shows the values as fetched at the time of the dataset version.
