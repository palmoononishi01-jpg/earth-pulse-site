CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry)

CO2 emissions from fossil fuel combustion (coal, oil, gas), cement production, flaring and other carbonates. Excludes land-use change and non-CO2 gases. International aviation and shipping are not allocated to countries.

View CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) on the globe

Definition

CO2 emissions from fossil fuel combustion (coal, oil, gas), cement production, flaring and other carbonates. Excludes land-use change and non-CO2 gases. International aviation and shipping are not allocated to countries.

Unit: Mt CO2

How to read this indicator

Annual emissions of carbon dioxide (CO2) from burning fossil fuels (coal, oil and gas) and from cement production and similar sources. The unit is million tonnes of CO2.

How it differs from similar indicators

CO2 per capita is the same emissions divided by population. The total depends heavily on the size (population) of the country, and the per-capita value evens out differences in population. Emissions from land-use change and greenhouse gases other than CO2 are not included.

Reading notes

  • It counts emissions from fuel burned within the country (its territory). Emissions embodied in imported goods are not included in a country's figure, and emissions from international aviation and shipping are not allocated to countries.
  • Countries and years without a value are "No data". They are not filled with 0 or estimates.
  • Providers sometimes revise past values. This site shows the values as fetched at the time of the dataset version.
