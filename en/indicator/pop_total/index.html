Population

Total residents regardless of legal status or citizenship (midyear estimate).

View Population on the globe

Definition

Total residents regardless of legal status or citizenship (midyear estimate).

Unit: people

How to read this indicator

The total number of people living in a country. Residents are counted regardless of citizenship or legal status, as a midyear estimate. It is not a census figure itself but a value based on national statistics and United Nations estimates.

How it differs from similar indicators

Population growth is the speed of change in population and population density is people per unit of area; both are different indicators from total population. Total population shows the size of a country, the other two show the speed of change and crowding.

Reading notes

  • It is a midyear estimate, so past values can change when a census is taken or the estimation method is revised. The treatment of refugees and people living abroad differs by country.
  • Countries and years without a value are "No data". They are not filled with 0 or estimates.
  • Providers sometimes revise past values. This site shows the values as fetched at the time of the dataset version.
