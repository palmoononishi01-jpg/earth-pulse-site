What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of Germany was about 1.2 times that of France (83.49M people and 68.72M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of Germany was about 1.5 times that of France ($5.051T and $3.366T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of Germany was about 1.2 times that of France ($60,496 and $48,986).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of Germany was about 2.2 times that of France (573.5 Mt CO2 and 255.4 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of Germany was about 1.8 times that of France (6.8 t CO2/person and 3.8 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 58.5% for Germany and 26.6% for France, a difference of 31.82 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 32.8% for Germany and 32.9% for France, a difference of 0.01 percentage points.
  • For Population, Germany was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), Germany was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), France was larger in 1985 and Germany was larger in 1986 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
