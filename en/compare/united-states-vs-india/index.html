What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of India was about 4.3 times that of United States (1.464B people and 341.8M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of United States was about 7.8 times that of India ($30.77T and $3.956T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of United States was about 33.3 times that of India ($90,027 and $2,702).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of United States was about 1.6 times that of India (5,036 Mt CO2 and 3,203 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of United States was about 6.6 times that of India (14.5 t CO2/person and 2.2 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 25.6% for United States and 24.6% for India, a difference of 1.08 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 33.8% for United States and 24.4% for India, a difference of 9.34 percentage points.
  • For Population, India was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), United States was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), United States was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
