What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of India was about 1 times that of China (1.464B people and 1.407B people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of China was about 4.9 times that of India ($19.5T and $3.956T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of China was about 5.1 times that of India ($13,862 and $2,702).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of China was about 3.9 times that of India (12,417 Mt CO2 and 3,203 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of China was about 4 times that of India (8.8 t CO2/person and 2.2 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 36.5% for China and 24.6% for India, a difference of 11.9 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 24.0% for China and 24.4% for India, a difference of 0.42 percentage points.
  • For Population, China was larger in 2020 and India was larger in 2021 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).
  • For GDP (current US$), India was larger in 1987 and China was larger in 1988 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), India was larger in 1990 and China was larger in 1991 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
