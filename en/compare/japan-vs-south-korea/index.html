What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of Japan was about 2.4 times that of South Korea (123.4M people and 51.68M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of Japan was about 2.4 times that of South Korea ($4.435T and $1.872T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of South Korea was about 1 times that of Japan ($36,227 and $35,951).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of Japan was about 1.7 times that of South Korea (960.2 Mt CO2 and 580.6 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of South Korea was about 1.4 times that of Japan (11.2 t CO2/person and 7.8 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 23.9% for Japan and 10.5% for South Korea, a difference of 13.44 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 68.3% for Japan and 64.4% for South Korea, a difference of 3.97 percentage points.
  • For Population, Japan was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), Japan was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), Japan was larger in 2022 and South Korea was larger in 2023 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
