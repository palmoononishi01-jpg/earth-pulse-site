What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of United Kingdom was about 1 times that of France (69.49M people and 68.72M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of United Kingdom was about 1.2 times that of France ($4.003T and $3.366T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of United Kingdom was about 1.2 times that of France ($57,602 and $48,986).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of United Kingdom was about 1.3 times that of France (319.9 Mt CO2 and 255.4 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of United Kingdom was about 1.2 times that of France (4.6 t CO2/person and 3.8 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 51.8% for United Kingdom and 26.6% for France, a difference of 25.18 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 13.5% for United Kingdom and 32.9% for France, a difference of 19.35 percentage points.
  • For Population, France was larger in 2022 and United Kingdom was larger in 2023 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).
  • For GDP (current US$), France was larger in 2013 and United Kingdom was larger in 2014 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), France was larger in 2011 and United Kingdom was larger in 2012 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
