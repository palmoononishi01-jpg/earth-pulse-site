What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of Japan was about 1.5 times that of Germany (123.4M people and 83.49M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of Germany was about 1.1 times that of Japan ($5.051T and $4.435T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of Germany was about 1.7 times that of Japan ($60,496 and $35,951).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of Japan was about 1.7 times that of Germany (960.2 Mt CO2 and 573.5 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of Japan was about 1.1 times that of Germany (7.8 t CO2/person and 6.8 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 23.9% for Japan and 58.5% for Germany, a difference of 34.57 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 68.3% for Japan and 32.8% for Germany, a difference of 35.5 percentage points.
  • For Population, Japan was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), Japan was larger in 2022 and Germany was larger in 2023 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), Japan was larger in 2012 and Germany was larger in 2013 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
