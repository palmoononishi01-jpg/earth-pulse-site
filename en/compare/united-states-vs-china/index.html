What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of China was about 4.1 times that of United States (1.407B people and 341.8M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of United States was about 1.6 times that of China ($30.77T and $19.5T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of United States was about 6.5 times that of China ($90,027 and $13,862).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of China was about 2.5 times that of United States (12,417 Mt CO2 and 5,036 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of United States was about 1.7 times that of China (14.5 t CO2/person and 8.8 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 25.6% for United States and 36.5% for China, a difference of 10.82 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 33.8% for United States and 24.0% for China, a difference of 9.76 percentage points.
  • For Population, China was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), United States was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), United States was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
