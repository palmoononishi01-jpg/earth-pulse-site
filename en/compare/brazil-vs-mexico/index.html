What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of Brazil was about 1.6 times that of Mexico (212.8M people and 131.9M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of Brazil was about 1.2 times that of Mexico ($2.28T and $1.833T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of Mexico was about 1.3 times that of Brazil ($13,889 and $10,713).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of Brazil was about 1.1 times that of Mexico (496.7 Mt CO2 and 470.9 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of Mexico was about 1.5 times that of Brazil (3.6 t CO2/person and 2.3 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 86.9% for Brazil and 21.6% for Mexico, a difference of 65.28 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 58.6% for Brazil and 34.2% for Mexico, a difference of 24.4 percentage points.
  • For Population, Brazil was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), Mexico was larger in 2005 and Brazil was larger in 2006 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), Brazil was larger in 2017 and Mexico was larger in 2018 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
