What the data shows directly

  • In 2025, Population of United States was about 2.8 times that of Japan (341.8M people and 123.4M people).
  • In 2025, GDP (current US$) of United States was about 6.9 times that of Japan ($30.77T and $4.435T).
  • In 2025, GDP per capita (current US$) of United States was about 2.5 times that of Japan ($90,027 and $35,951).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry) of United States was about 5.2 times that of Japan (5,036 Mt CO2 and 960.2 Mt CO2).
  • In 2025, CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry) of United States was about 1.9 times that of Japan (14.5 t CO2/person and 7.8 t CO2/person).
  • In 2025, Renewables share of electricity generation was 23.9% for Japan and 25.6% for United States, a difference of 1.75 percentage points.
  • In 2024, Forest area (% of land area) was 68.3% for Japan and 33.8% for United States, a difference of 34.56 percentage points.
  • For Population, United States was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP (current US$), United States was larger throughout 1960–2025, the years in which both countries have a value.
  • For GDP per capita (current US$), Japan was larger in 2000 and United States was larger in 2001 (the most recent switch, looking at years in which both have a value).

Ratios and differences are not calculated for indicators where the two countries have no common year with values.
