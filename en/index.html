EARTH PULSE

What is happening on Earth right now?

EARTH PULSE is a free service for exploring world statistics on population, the economy, energy and the environment, together with natural events such as earthquakes and wildfires, on a 3D globe and a world map. No sign-up is required.

Open the globe

Free, no sign-up, works on smartphones.

What you can do here

  • See 13 indicators such as population, GDP, CO2 emissions, the renewable share of electricity and forest area, colored year by year on a world map and a globe.
  • Pick a country to see its latest values, world rank and trend. You can also compare two to five countries.
  • Overlay near real-time earthquakes (USGS) and volcanoes, wildfires, storms and more (NASA EONET) on the globe. These are not official warnings.
  • Every number comes with its year and source. Countries and years without data are shown as "No data".
