How to read this ranking

  • The year is 2024, the latest year in which at least 90% of the maximum number of countries have a value (the latest year with any value is 2025).
  • "194 countries" is the number of countries with a value in this year. Ranks are positions among those countries, not among every country in the world.
  • Countries with equal values share a rank. "Rank 10 years earlier" is the rank for the same indicator in 2014; it is "—" if the country had no value that year.
  • A larger or smaller value does not mean good or bad. Differences in the size of countries, such as population and area, also matter.

What the indicator means and how to compare

Share of total electricity generation from renewables (hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy, other renewables). Excludes nuclear. Electricity only — not the share of total final energy consumption (World Bank EG.FEC.RNEW.ZS).

The share (%) of a country's total electricity generation that comes from renewables such as hydro, wind, solar and bioenergy.

It is a different indicator from the share of renewables in total energy consumption (World Bank EG.FEC.RNEW.ZS). This indicator covers electricity generation only, and nuclear power is not counted as renewable.

It is a share of generation, so it does not reflect the size of electricity demand or the amount imported or exported. Many countries have not yet published a value for the latest year.
