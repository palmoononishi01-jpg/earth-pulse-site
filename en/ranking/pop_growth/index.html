How to read this ranking

What the indicator means and how to compare

Exponential rate of growth of midyear population from year t-1 to t, in percent. Negative values mean decline.

How much the population grew or shrank compared with the previous year, as an annual percentage. A negative value means the population declined.

Total population is the number of people itself, while population growth is the speed of change (percent). A populous country can have a low growth rate, and a country with a high growth rate can add few people.

In countries with small populations, or where migration or refugee flows are large, the value can swing widely from year to year.
