How to read this ranking

What the indicator means and how to compare

Calculated by EARTH PULSE as total population divided by land area of the same year. Not calculated when either input is missing (no interpolation).

People per square kilometer of land. A calculated value: this site divides total population by land area.

It divides by land area, which leaves out inland water such as lakes and large rivers, not by the whole territory. For some countries and years it differs from the density published by the World Bank (EN.POP.DNST) because of differences in coverage.

It is a national average, so it does not show how population is spread within a country, such as the gap between cities and rural areas. City-states and small island countries have extremely large values.
