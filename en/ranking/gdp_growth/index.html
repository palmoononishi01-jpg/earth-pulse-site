How to read this ranking

  • The year is 2024, the latest year in which at least 90% of the maximum number of countries have a value (the latest year with any value is 2025).
  • "200 countries" is the number of countries with a value in this year. Ranks are positions among those countries, not among every country in the world.
  • Countries with equal values share a rank. "Rank 10 years earlier" is the rank for the same indicator in 2014; it is "—" if the country had no value that year.
  • A larger or smaller value does not mean good or bad. Differences in the size of countries, such as population and area, also matter.

What the indicator means and how to compare

Annual percentage growth rate of GDP at market prices based on constant local currency.

How many percent real GDP, with price changes removed, grew compared with the previous year. It is calculated in local currency at constant prices. A negative value means negative growth.

Nominal GDP (an amount) includes the effect of prices and exchange rates, whereas this indicator is a growth rate with price effects removed. Because it is a rate of change rather than an amount, countries of different sizes can be placed side by side, but it does not show the size of the economy.

The year after a sharp downturn can show a high value as a rebound. Values for each year can be revised.
