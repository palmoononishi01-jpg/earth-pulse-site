Definitions of the indicators

The indicators in the table above are defined as follows (based on the definitions of the World Bank, the Global Carbon Project and Ember).

Population
Total residents regardless of legal status or citizenship (midyear estimate).
Population growth (annual %)
Exponential rate of growth of midyear population from year t-1 to t, in percent. Negative values mean decline.
Population density (per km² of land area)
Calculated by EARTH PULSE as total population divided by land area of the same year. Not calculated when either input is missing (no interpolation).
Land area
Country's total area excluding area under inland water bodies and national claims to continental shelf and exclusive economic zones.
GDP (current US$)
Gross domestic product converted to US dollars at annual official exchange rates (current prices).
GDP per capita (current US$)
GDP divided by midyear population (current US$).
GDP growth (annual %)
Annual percentage growth rate of GDP at market prices based on constant local currency.
CO2 emissions (fossil fuels and industry)
CO2 emissions from fossil fuel combustion (coal, oil, gas), cement production, flaring and other carbonates. Excludes land-use change and non-CO2 gases. International aviation and shipping are not allocated to countries.
CO2 emissions per capita (fossil fuels and industry)
Fossil and industry CO2 emissions per person (t CO2 per capita), as published by the Global Carbon Project (using its own population series). Different from World Bank EN.ATM.CO2E.PC (archived) and EN.GHG.CO2.PC.CE.AR5 (EDGAR).
Renewables share of electricity generation
Share of total electricity generation from renewables (hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy, other renewables). Excludes nuclear. Electricity only — not the share of total final energy consumption (World Bank EG.FEC.RNEW.ZS).
Electricity demand
Electricity demand (generation plus net imports), in terawatt-hours.
Electricity demand per capita
Calculated by EARTH PULSE: Ember electricity demand divided by World Bank population of the same year (kWh per person). Different from World Bank EG.USE.ELEC.KH.PC (IEA electric power consumption).
Forest area (% of land area)
Land under natural or planted stands of trees of at least 5 meters, as a share of land area (excludes trees in agricultural production and urban parks).

Reading notes

  • The latest published year differs by indicator, so values for different years sit in one table. Always check the year.
  • A rank counts only the countries that have a value for that indicator in that year. It is not necessarily a rank among every country in the world.
  • "Calculated" means this site computed the value from other indicators. "No data" means the source has no value (it is not zero).
  • Money values are nominal US dollars. They are not adjusted for prices or purchasing power.
