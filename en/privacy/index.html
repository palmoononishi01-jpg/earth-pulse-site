Privacy policy

Operator: [NOT SET] · Last revised: 2026-11-23

[NOT SET] (the "Operator") sets out below how information is handled on EARTH PULSE, a free 3D globe service (the "Site").

Information we collect and what is stored on your device

The Site has no sign-up, login or submission form, and the Operator does not store personal information on its server.

Only the following is saved inside your browser (localStorage). It stays on your device and is not sent to the Operator's server. It is not a cookie.

  • Display settings such as graphics quality
  • The time you marked natural events as read (used to show what is new)
  • The language you chose

Server access logs

The server that delivers the Site automatically records access (IP address, date and time, the URL viewed, browser type, referrer and so on). The Operator may use these records to investigate failures, prevent abuse and measure usage.

Advertising

The Site does not currently use advertising. This page will be updated before any advertising starts.

Analytics

The Site does not currently use an analytics tool (tag). Usage may be understood only from tallies of the server access logs.

Communication with other servers

The statistics, maps and natural event data shown on the globe are loaded from the Site's own server. The globe does not call the data providers' APIs directly.

A share button opens the posting screen of X or another service in a new window only when you press it. Nothing is sent to them before that. When you follow a link to an outside site, such as a source, that site's own policy applies.

Provision to third parties

The Operator does not provide users' information to third parties except where required by law.
