About EARTH PULSE

What this service is

EARTH PULSE is a free information service for exploring world statistics and natural events on a 3D globe and a world map. It aims to let you check, by switching countries and indicators yourself, what is happening on Earth right now.

Explore in the globe app, then read the definition and source of each number calmly on the country, indicator, ranking and comparison pages.

Open the globe

How data is handled

  • Statistics are open data from the World Bank, the Global Carbon Project and Ember. Sources and licenses are listed on the Data sources page.
  • Every number carries its year and source. Countries and years without data show "No data" and are never filled with 0 or estimates.
  • Values this site calculates from other indicators (such as population density) are labeled "Calculated".
  • Page text consists of handwritten definitions and notes plus factual sentences computed from the data (templates). No causes, evaluations or forecasts are written.

Data sources

What it does not do

  • It gives no disaster warnings or forecasts. The display of natural events is not an official warning.
  • It does not judge which country is better or worse. It only orders values by size.
  • The way the map is drawn does not express a political position.

Languages

Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese and Spanish are supported. Switch from the top of any page.
